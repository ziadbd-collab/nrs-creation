About Studio

The Visionary Behind NRS Creation

Shaikh Ziadul Haque

Founder, Multidisciplinary Artist, Writer & Playwright

Shaikh Ziadul Haque is a versatile creative force deeply rooted in modern digital production, narrative arts, and performance. Associated with renowned theater groups like Aranyak Natyadal, his artistic vision blends psychological depth with futuristic concepts.

Through NRS Creation, he directs high-end 3D visual effects, writes thought-provoking stage plays, publishes visionary literature, and crafts striking abstract wall art collections.
