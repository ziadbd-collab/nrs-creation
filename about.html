Shaikh Ziadul Haque

Author, Playwright, Entrepreneur & Social Activist

Shaikh Ziadul Haque is a versatile creative professional who has established a distinct identity as an author, playwright, corporate professional, and digital entrepreneur. At 44 years of age, every facet of his work is driven by a deep sense of social responsibility and an innovative perspective.

Literary & Theatrical Pursuits

Shaikh Ziadul Haque is a dedicated playwright and author. He is an active member of the renowned theatre troupe Aranyak Natyadal, where he contributes to the development of the performing arts. His plays—such as Alpin, Amar Desher Mukh, Badsha, Bosoti, and Badyajantri—critically reflect on social injustices, humanitarian crises, and the complex impacts of technology.

Additionally, he has adapted his works into English: Mangal Barta as "Hawking is on Mars" and Jouktikotay Jukto as "Connected in Logic". He is also the author of the book "We Are Responsible" and an Inspirational book series. His literary works are available on his Amazon Author Page.

Professional Life & Social Activism

Professionally, Shaikh Ziadul Haque is employed in a private company, where he continues to demonstrate his professional commitment. As part of his social consciousness, he serves as an online member of Transparency International, working toward a transparent society and raising his voice against corruption.

Furthermore, he is planning to establish the 'Harun Nouri Foundation,' a project that reflects his humanitarian vision.

Digital Entrepreneurship & Creative Media

As a digital entrepreneur, he is active on platforms like Amazon KDP and manages his own branding and merchandise initiatives through his Creator Spring (TeeSpring) store. He is skilled in 3D modeling, animation, and video editing using Blender, which is showcased on his NRS Creation YouTube channel. Balancing his career, artistic pursuits, and business ventures, he constantly strives to enrich the landscape of literature and digital art.
